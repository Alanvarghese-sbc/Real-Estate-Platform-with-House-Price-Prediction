User Journey:
1. User logs in
2. Clicks "Add Property"
3. Fills form (District, Area, Bedrooms, Location, Price)
4. Submits form
5. ✨ ML automatically predicts price (invisible to user)
6. Property saved with both listed and predicted prices

Broker Journey:
1. Broker logs in
2. Views property bids
3. Sees AI predicted price alongside user bids
4. Makes informed decision based on:

   - AI predicted fair price
   - Market conditions
